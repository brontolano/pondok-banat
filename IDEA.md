A digital platform for managing Islamic boarding schools (pesantren) for girls, streamlining administration, curriculum, and guardian communication.

- Digitize student registration, attendance, and academic records with role-based access for admins, teachers, and parents.  
- Provide a structured curriculum builder for Qur’an memorization, Arabic, and general studies with progress tracking.  
- Enable secure messaging and real-time notifications between guardians and dorm supervisors.  
- Generate automated reports for tuition, health logs, and disciplinary actions.  
- Support offline-first mobile access for areas with limited connectivity.
